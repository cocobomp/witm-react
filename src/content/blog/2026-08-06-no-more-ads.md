# v3.2 — No More Ads, Honest Ties, and a Game Two People Can Start

WITM had ads. It doesn't any more.

## The Ads Are Gone

Not hidden, not reduced — gone. And with them, the consent form that greeted every new player before their first game.

The honest reason is arithmetic. At the size WITM is today, ads brought in roughly three francs a month. The interstitial sat exactly across the moment players decide whether to start another game, and the consent form was the first thing a new player saw. Three francs is not worth either of those.

There is a detail worth telling, because it took a while to find. The ads were already switchable from the server — one flag, no app update needed. But the consent form was not wired to that flag: it was shown to anyone who *could* be shown an ad, whether or not any ad would ever come. So the app could switch its ads off and go on asking people for permission to personalise the ads it had just stopped serving. That is fixed. One switch now governs both, and it reaches phones that have already been installed.

## A Tie Is a Tie

Two players on the same number of votes used to be quietly ranked, one above the other, as though the game knew something it didn't.

Now they share a rank, they share the crown, and a round that ends level pays nobody. It sounds small. It is the difference between a scoreboard you argue with and one you trust.

## Who Voted For Whom — With Names

The results screen showed the voters as a row of little portraits. That reads fine until two friends kept the default avatar, at which point the recap becomes unreadable in exactly the moment it exists for.

It says names now. And when the screen is too narrow to hold both, it drops the portraits rather than the names — the portraits were repeating the same people anyway.

## Two Players Are Enough

New defaults: five rounds instead of ten, votes visible, and voting for yourself allowed. That last one is why two players can now start a game. Turn self-voting off in the settings and you're back to needing three.

Five rounds instead of ten because ten was a commitment. Five is a round of drinks.

## It Holds at Any Text Size

If you use large text on your phone, parts of WITM used to fight you. Names ran outside their cards in the lobby. A five-way tie showed two winners and silently kept three to itself. The category grid did something worse than ignore your setting: it *shrank* the labels the bigger you asked for them.

That's about thirty separate fixes, each one measured rather than eyeballed, across every screen from the lobby to the final standings. If you don't use large text you will notice nothing, which is the point.

## Also

- Fixed a crash in Top 1 when the keyboard opened at a large text size
- Leaving the end-of-game screen now tells the room you've left, so nobody sits there waiting for a rematch from a host who has gone home
