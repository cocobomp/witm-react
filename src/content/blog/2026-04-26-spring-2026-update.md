# Spring 2026 Update: Vote Integrity, Accessibility, and Quiet Polish

Some updates ship a flashy new feature. This one is different. Over the past three weeks we ran a deep audit of the app — security, accessibility, performance, code health — and shipped 25 fixes. Most of them you'll never notice. And that's the point.

Here's what changed.

## Your Votes Are Now Tamper-Proof

When you rate a question 👍 or 👎 in a room, that rating used to live in the room itself — in a shared list anyone in the room could technically rewrite. The data was never actually abused, but a determined player on a modded client could have inflated their own counts or overwritten yours.

Not anymore. Each rating now lives in a per-user, per-question record protected directly by the database. The rule is enforced at the storage layer:

> A player can only write their own rating. They cannot touch yours, ever.

Older app versions still work normally — they keep using the previous system, and the new app reads both sources transparently. No friction.

## Every Button Now Hits 44px

We went screen by screen and made sure every tappable element matches Apple's and Google's accessibility minimum: **44 × 44 points**. Smaller buttons got padding. Tightly packed icons got breathing room. The lobby, the rating bar, the in-game reactions — all touched up.

Color contrast also got a pass. Borderline ratios were nudged above the WCAG AA threshold (4.5:1 for body text). Reading the app should now be a little easier on the eyes — especially in bright daylight.

## Translations, Properly Finished

A few error messages were still hardcoded in English ("Not enough players to start" and friends). All of those are now localized in **English, French, and German** — including the couple mode, wedding mode, and party mode flows.

If your app is set to French or German, you shouldn't see English strings sneak in anymore.

## Back-Compat, Quietly Strong

A subtle but important shift: we made sure everything we ship plays nicely with older app versions still in the wild. If a friend hasn't updated yet and joins your room, the game continues without errors. Rules, data formats, and message flows are all designed to be forward-compatible.

You wouldn't notice this work — but the whole point is that *nothing* breaks for anyone, ever.

## Smaller Things

- The splash screen no longer occasionally double-navigates after sign-in
- The leaderboard correctly handles up to 499 players in a single batch
- Voting is now atomic — no more rare double-counted votes when two phones vote at the same instant
- A handful of memory leaks in animation handlers are gone

## What's Next

This was a foundation update — locking down trust and polish before the next round of features. Stay tuned.

In the meantime: update your app on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ch/app/witm-who-is-the-most/id6740246093) or [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.qelp.ch), gather your friends, and play with peace of mind.
