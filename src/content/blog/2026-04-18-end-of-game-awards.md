# End-of-Game Awards: Meet the Mouton, the Rebel, and Three More

The best part of a party game isn't the score — it's the post-game debrief. *"Wait, you voted for ME on that one?!"*

We wanted to capture that moment. So every game of Who Is The Most now ends with **Awards** — fun titles handed out based on how everyone voted.

## Five Awards, One Trophy Ceremony

At the end of every game, we analyze the full voting history and hand out up to five titles. Each one is earned — not random.

- **L'Unanime** 🎯 — The question where one player got the highest % of votes. When the room agrees, the room *really* agrees.

- **Le Controversé** 🔥 — The most evenly split question of the game. The one that made everyone hesitate.

- **Le Mouton** 🐑 — The player who voted with the majority the most often. Team player or social chameleon? Up to you to decide.

- **Le Rebelle** ✊ — The player who voted *against* the majority the most often. Either brave, or wrong. Often both.

- **Le Narcissique** 🪞 — The player who voted for themselves the most often. Only appears when self-voting is enabled. Shame optional.

## Why It Matters

Scores tell you who "won." Awards tell you **how everyone played**. The group clown, the contrarian, the peacekeeper — they all get their moment at the end.

And because each player only gets one award max, even the quiet players walk away with a title. No one leaves empty-handed.

## Behind the Scenes

Awards are calculated locally from the full vote log — no extra server calls, no delay. You see them the moment the last round ends, revealed one by one with a little staggered animation.

We also made sure the algorithm handles tie-breaking cleanly: if two players qualify for the same award, priority goes to the rarer category (Unanime > Controversé > Mouton > Rebelle > Narcissique). You won't see the same person win two awards in one game.
