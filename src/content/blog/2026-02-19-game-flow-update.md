# Mid-Game Rankings, Custom Lists & Rematch

This is one of our biggest gameplay updates yet. We've added features that make every game more competitive, more personal, and way more replayable. Let's break it down!

## Live Rankings Between Rounds

After each round, the host can now choose to show the **standings screen** — a live leaderboard that tracks cumulative scores throughout the game. You'll see:

- **Everyone ranked** by their current score (3 pts for 1st, 2 pts for 2nd, 1 pt for 3rd)
- **Rank change indicators** showing who moved up or down since the last round
- **Player avatars and names** for that competitive visual flair

The host controls when to move on by pressing **Continue**, so there's time to celebrate (or trash-talk) before the next question drops.

Want to skip the standings? The **Skip** button goes directly to the next question — no ranking screen in between.

## Play with Your Own Questions

This is the one you've been asking for. You can now create **custom question lists** and use them in your games! Head to the "Custom" category when creating a room, select your personal lists, and play with questions that only your group will get.

Inside jokes, niche topics, themed parties — the possibilities are endless.

## Instant Rematch

Game over? Don't stop! The host can hit **Play Again** right from the leaderboard to create a rematch room with the same settings. Other players see a **Join Rematch** button and get into the new lobby with one tap. No more sharing PINs again — it just works.

## Smoother Timer Sync

We've rewritten the countdown timer to use **millisecond-precision sync** across all devices. Everyone now sees the same second at the same time — no more "I had 3 seconds left but you had 1!" moments.

## Confetti for Winners Only

The confetti celebration now plays **only on the winner's device**. If you topped the round, you'll feel it — with haptic feedback and a burst of confetti. Everyone else just sees who won. It makes winning feel special.

## Quick PIN Join

Joining a room is now even faster — just type the 3-digit PIN and you're in. No need to tap a Join button anymore; it submits automatically after the last digit.

## What's Next

We're just getting started. More ways to customize your games, new question categories, and social features are all on the roadmap. Stay tuned!
