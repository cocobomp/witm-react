# Collaborative Questions: Your Friends Write the Game

What if the best questions didn't come from us — but from your friends? That's exactly the idea behind **Collaborative Questions**, our brand-new game mode that turns the lobby into a creative playground.

## What Are Collaborative Questions?

Instead of relying only on our question bank, everyone in the lobby can now **submit their own questions** before the game starts. Think of it as a group brainstorm session — except nobody knows what anyone else wrote until the questions pop up mid-game.

The result? Surprises, inside jokes, and some seriously chaotic rounds.

## How to Enable It

When creating a room, you'll see a new **Collaborative Mode** toggle. Flip it on, and a dedicated question panel appears in the lobby for all players.

That's it — no complicated setup, no extra menus. One toggle and you're good to go.

## How It Works

Once Collaborative Mode is active, every player in the lobby gets access to a **question submission panel**. Type your question, hit submit, and it gets added to the pool. Here's what you need to know:

- **Questions are anonymous** — the lobby only shows a count of how many questions have been submitted, not the content. The surprise factor is part of the fun.
- **Collab questions mix with category questions** — the game doesn't replace anything. Your total question pool = category questions + collaborative questions.
- **Max 30 collaborative questions** per game, with a minimum of 10 characters per question. No one-word trolling allowed (well, almost).
- **The host or the question's author can delete** any collaborative question — so there's always a safety net.

## Works with Custom Lists Too

Already using custom question lists? Great news — Collaborative Mode works **alongside** them. Turn on both, and your game pulls from your custom lists, the selected categories, AND whatever your friends come up with on the spot. Maximum variety, maximum fun.

## Why You'll Love It

This feature was built for one reason: to make your games **feel like yours**. Pre-made questions are fun, but nothing beats the moment when a question pops up and the entire room bursts out laughing because it's clearly about something that happened last weekend.

Birthday parties, friend groups, team outings, family gatherings — Collaborative Questions turns every session into something truly unique. Nobody plays the same game twice.

## Try It Tonight

Collaborative Questions is available now. Update your app, create a room, toggle on Collaborative Mode, and let your friends do the rest.

Trust us — the questions they'll come up with are way better than anything we could write.
