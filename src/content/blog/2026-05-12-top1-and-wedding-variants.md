# v3.0 — Top 1 Mode, Wedding in Two Flavors, and the L'Emojeur Award

Three months after Couple mode, here's v3.0 — the biggest update since WITM launched. A new game mode, a new wedding variant, a new end-of-game award, and polish everywhere.

## Top 1 Mode — Speed Decides

Until now, you voted. A "who is the most likely to…" prompt would appear, you'd pick a player, and the most-voted answer won. It was fun, but deeply social — the app mostly framed a conversation.

**Top 1 mode flips the dynamic entirely.** A question appears, and you type the answer. The first player to type the correct answer scores the points. No voting, no consensus — just your reflexes and what's in your head.

It's faster, sharper, more addictive. Perfect for bus rides, tense apéros, or evenings where players don't know each other well enough to vote on each other yet.

## Wedding Mode, Now in Two Flavors

Wedding mode exploded after launch: bachelorette/bachelor parties, rehearsal dinners, full weddings — feedback poured in. The most common request? *"We'd love something faster for long evenings."*

So now, when you create a Wedding game, you pick:

- **Crigui** — the classic version: vote together, with a new three-segment vote bar that highlights the "both" option in gold
- **Top 1** — the new trivia mode, wedding flavor, game-show vibes

You can alternate the two through the evening to switch up the pace. The Crigui catalog grew to **27 questions** (up from 20), and you can now play with **up to 50 guests** at once.

## The L'Emojeur Award

At the end of each game, automatic awards are already handed out: The Sheep, The Rebel, The Target, and others. v3.0 adds a new one: **L'Emojeur**, for the player who sent the most emoji reactions during the game.

It's our way of rewarding the players who *animate* games — not the ones who win the most, but the ones who react the most. Often it's the same person. Often it's not.

## Small Polish You Shouldn't Miss

- **Game resume prompt**: the app now asks before pulling you back into an ongoing game, instead of catapulting you in without warning
- **Outdated-client notice**: if a friend has a version too old to join your room, they get a clear message instead of a silent failure
- **Accessibility**: wider touch targets, refined contrast
- **Polished FR/DE translations**: a few phrasings and terms cleaned up
