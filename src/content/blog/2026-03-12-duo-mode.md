# DUO Mode: A New Way to Play — Just the Two of You

Who Is The Most has always been about groups — the more people, the more chaos. But we kept hearing the same request: *"Can we play with just two people?"*

Today, the answer is yes. Say hello to **DUO mode**.

## What Is DUO Mode?

DUO is a brand-new category designed for **two players only**. No need for a full group — grab your partner, your date, or your best friend, and start a game in seconds.

But DUO isn't just "the same game with fewer people." We built **60 entirely new questions** that only make sense when there are two of you in the room.

## Three Vibes, One Mode

When you select DUO, you choose a **vibe** that sets the tone for the entire session:

- **Date** — Light, fun, flirty. Perfect for a first date or getting to know someone better. Questions like *"Who's more likely to text first?"* or *"Who would plan a surprise trip?"*

- **Couple** — Deeper, more personal. For people who already know each other well and want to see how aligned they really are. Think *"Who's more likely to forget an anniversary?"* or *"Who handles stress better?"*

- **Intimacy** — Bold, spicy, no filter. For couples who aren't afraid of honest answers. We'll let your imagination fill in the blanks on this one.

Each vibe has **20 dedicated questions** — no overlap, no recycled content. We wrote every single one from scratch.

## How to Play

1. Open the app and tap **Create a game**
2. Select the **DUO** category
3. Pick your vibe: Date, Couple, or Intimacy
4. Share the PIN with your partner
5. Start the game once they join

That's it. Same simple flow, new experience.

## Why We Built This

Party games are great for groups, but some of the best conversations happen one-on-one. DUO mode brings that energy into the game — whether you're on a date night, a road trip, or just hanging out on the couch.

It's also a great icebreaker. If you're meeting someone new and want to skip the small talk, DUO Date mode does the heavy lifting for you.
